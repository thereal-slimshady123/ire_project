7235 held-out queries, 13 datasets (published Experiment 2d predictions; hybrid = per-fold tuned α). ✅ = 95% CI above 0, ❌ = below 0. `Niche?` = BM25 or dense beats hybrid on average in that group (CI > 0); Holm-adjusted across the groups in each table.

## 1. Hindsight headroom (per query, not identifiable in advance)

- Oracle − hybrid: **+0.0432** nDCG@10 pooled.
- Queries where BM25 or dense beats hybrid at all: **22.2%**; by ≥ 0.10: **13.5%**; by ≥ 0.20: **8.8%**.
- Queries where hybrid is worse than *both* single retrievers: 0.7%.
