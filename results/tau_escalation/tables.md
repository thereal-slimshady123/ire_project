# Router + confidence-threshold (τ) escalation, end to end

Exploratory, not pre-registered. τ is calibrated per fold on out-of-fold *training* router probabilities (inner 3-fold CV) to hit each target rate; held-out rates are what the threshold actually produces. Escalation = Experiment 4's MiniLM-L6 rerank of the hybrid top-50. Random controls escalate at the same realized rate (expected value). nDCG@10.

Held-out GBM routes reproduce Experiment 2 on 95.5% of queries.
